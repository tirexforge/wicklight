Privacy Policy – Wicklight

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Android app Wicklight (the "Game"), published by Tirex Forge Games (Francesco Tirello) ("the Developer", "we"). For any privacy question, contact tirexforge@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or other personal data.
  • Your progress (high score, missions, skins, upgrades, Sparks) is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may process some device data (see section 4).
  • Where required (European Economic Area, United Kingdom) we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change it at any time.

3. Data stored on your device

To work, the Game stores on your device: your distance record, missions and daily challenges, Sparks, unlocked skins and upgrades, your daily login streak and settings. This data stays in the app's private storage: we do not receive it and we do not send it to any server. You can delete it at any time from Options → Reset progress or by uninstalling the Game.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show ads, in particular rewarded ads you may choose to watch to get an extra life or Sparks. Watching is always optional.

To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect and process, through the SDK built into the Game, information such as:

  • the device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and other device or installation identifiers;
  • the IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the device, operating system, app and network;
  • data about ad interactions (impressions, clicks, completion).

Google processes this data as an independent controller under its own policies. More information: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can limit ad personalisation in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads), where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID.

5. Consent (EU, EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, on first launch the Game may show a consent form (provided by Google's User Messaging Platform) where you choose whether to allow the use of your data for personalised ads. If you decline you can still play and will see non-personalised ads. You can review or withdraw your choice at any time from Options → Ad consent, when that item is available.

6. Legal basis

Processing for personalised ads is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time without affecting earlier processing. Local saving of your progress is necessary to provide the Game.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent. Since we do not receive your personal data, for data processed by Google for ads you can exercise these rights directly with Google. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

9. Retention and security

Game data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Game. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to its policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the Game or the rules change. The date above shows the last update; for significant changes we will notify you in the Game or on the store page.
