Privacy Policy – Wicklight

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Android app Wicklight (the "Game"), published by Tirex Forge Games (Francesco Tirello) ("the Developer", "we"). For any privacy question, contact tirexforge@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or other personal data.
  • Your progress (high score, missions, skins, upgrades, Sparks) is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may process some device data (see section 4).
  • Where required (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland) we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change it at any time.

3. Data stored on your device

To work, the Game stores on your device: your distance record, missions and daily challenges, Sparks, unlocked skins and upgrades, your daily login streak and settings. This data stays in the app's private storage: we do not receive it and we do not send it to any server. You can delete it at any time from Options → Reset progress or by uninstalling the Game.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include in your message. We use this data only to reply to your request and manage the related communication.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show ads, in particular rewarded ads you may choose to watch to get an extra life or Sparks. Watching is always optional.

To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may collect and process, through the SDK built into the Game, information such as:

  • the device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and other device or installation identifiers;
  • the IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • information about the device, operating system, app and network;
  • data about ad interactions (impressions, clicks, completion).
  • information about interaction with the app, such as app launches and taps;
  • diagnostic and performance information about the app and the SDK, such as launch time, hangs and energy usage.
  • the App Set ID (an identifier tied to apps from the same developer);

For processing connected to AdMob, the Developer and Google act as independent controllers, each for its own processing; Google processes the data under its own terms and notices. More information: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Google and its partners may process data in countries other than the one where you are: Google describes the mechanisms it uses for international transfers (including, where applicable, adequacy decisions, the Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses) on this page. The list of applicable ad technology providers is available in the consent form shown by the app and may vary depending on the AdMob configuration.

You can limit ad personalisation in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads), where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID.

5. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, on first launch the Game may show a consent form (provided by Google's User Messaging Platform) where you choose whether to accept or decline the use of your data for personalised ads. If you decline, you can still play; any ads shown will be handled according to your consent choices and applicable law and may be non-personalised or limited ads. You can review or withdraw your choice at any time from Options → Ad consent, when that item is available.

6. Legal basis

Processing for personalised ads is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, or the equivalent provisions of UK and Swiss law, where applicable), which you can withdraw at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal. Emails you send us are processed to reply to your request and manage the related communication, on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or to comply with legal obligations. The progress data described in section 3 stays only on your device and is not transmitted to us.

7. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over, or older where the law of your country sets a higher minimum age for consenting to the processing of personal data (up to 16 in some countries); in that case use it only with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not verify users' age. The Game is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. For users whom Google identifies as minors, Google may limit personalised ads under its own policies and applicable law. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Under the data protection law that applies to you (for example the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Swiss law or other local laws) you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict the processing of, and object to the processing of your data, to data portability and to withdraw your consent. Where the GDPR applies, these rights include those under Arts. 15-22. For data Google processes for advertising, which we do not directly receive, you can exercise them with Google. If you contact us by email, we will respond to requests about the data we hold. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali; a list of EU authorities is on the EDPB website).

9. Retention and security

Game data is kept in the Game's private storage on the device, is excluded from Android backups (cloud and device-to-device transfer) and is not transmitted to our servers; it stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Game. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (TLS) under the security measures adopted by Google, which retains it according to its own policies. We keep emails we receive for as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up, unless further retention is necessary to comply with legal obligations or to protect our rights.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the Game or applicable laws or regulations change. The date above shows the last update; for significant changes we will notify you in the Game or on the store page. If the versions in different languages differ, the Italian version prevails, without prejudice to mandatory rights under applicable law.
